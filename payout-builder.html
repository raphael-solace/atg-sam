How the builder models the pools

The coverage builder adds picks to maximize the chance of covering all eight winners. The payout-aware builder simulates winners from the same forecasts and estimates how many public rows would share each of the four prize tiers. It swaps selections to improve estimated cash return within each ticket’s original leg widths. The selective policy also tries smaller systems and only plays when a separate simulation projects at least a 5% margin.

The opponent estimates, turnover model and payout cap were frozen from 2025-10-25 through 2025-12-13. The simulated payouts use a conservative cap to limit sensitivity to very rare outcomes. Every played ticket is scored using the historical dividends ATG actually posted, across five through eight correct.

Three LiteLLM routes forecast from anonymized form fields and final V85 shares. Later individual leg win odds were omitted. When a forecast could not be validated, both builders used the same public shares for that entry. The ensemble averages the three distributions. The archive does not provide a time series of prices and scratchings before V85 closed.

Pool estimates assume public rows can be approximated from the individual leg shares. The 15-round sample and the simulated payout model limit conclusions about future returns.
