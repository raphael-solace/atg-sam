How the comparison works
  • Orchestrator3 gives the public V85 shares at least 50% weight and fits the remaining blend of model and public forecasts using strictly earlier rounds.
  • Every forecast runs through the same coverage and payout-aware policies. The selective policy plays only when projected payout exceeds stake by 5%. Voluntary passes cost 0 SEK. Forecast failures use the public share distribution and are counted separately.
  • 10,000 randomized ticket portfolios draw runners from the public shares while matching each policy’s actual weeks played, ticket widths and stake. This isolates the runner selections from bet timing. The fraction matching or beating the result is conditional on the archived settlements.
  • Forecast skill is the percentage reduction in log loss versus public V85 shares. Orchestrator3’s 95% interval was -0.13% to +0.08%. Bootstrap intervals resample whole scored rounds. Archived shares, runner statistics and scratchings have limited timing metadata.
  • The forecasts came from LiteLLM. The extended benchmark does not use individual-leg win odds. June 20 had no V85 Saturday round.
