What changed in the prompt

Treat the ticket as a purchase, not as a prize for covering as many wins as possible. Begin by comparing the public V85 distribution with normalized inverse win odds. Correct major disagreements only when the independent signals or pre-race records support the correction. Keep probability shifts small when the evidence is noisy. Use the same probabilities to build your ticket, with extra coverage concentrated where the market appears to underprice plausible winners. Prioritize a lean ticket. Choose an explicit count for each of the eight legs before naming runners. Prefer a promising smaller ticket to a larger set of expensive combinations that mostly duplicate the crowd. Most legs should have at most three picks; use a fourth only for a leg with several plausible winners. Preserve some chances at the lower prize tiers. Check total rows and stake after your final selections and keep the cost under 500 SEK.

2026-06-20 was excluded because ATG calendar lists no V85 round.

Tuning ran from 2026-04-04 through 2026-05-23. The holdout ran from 2026-05-30 through 2026-09-05. The draft with the larger median model-level net improvement on tuning rounds was frozen before holdout scoring. On tuning rounds the median change was −193 SEK for the payout-aware draft and +407 SEK for selective value.

The four models received the original prompt or the same prompt with the selected instructions added. The Luna council used the same engine and ticket builder in both arms, with upstream forecasts from the corresponding prompt and expert selection based on earlier rounds.

Historical settlement does not establish future profitability. Archived final win odds for later legs may reflect information after V85 ticket sales closed. No real bets were placed.
